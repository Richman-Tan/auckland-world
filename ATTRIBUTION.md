- **Buildings, coastline, roads, landuse, place names:** © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the [Open Database Licence (ODbL)](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright). The derived map data in `public/map/` (building footprints and heights, ground texture, minimap, place list) is a derived database and is likewise available under the ODbL.
